Research pillars

Three questions the lab keeps returning to, across five years of empirical work.

01
Cognitive load

How much a developer must hold in mind to make a change safely — and what tooling adds to that budget rather than spending it.

02
Psychological safety

Whether an engineer can say "I do not understand this" in a review thread without paying for it later.

03
Socio-technical dynamics

Where the shape of a team and the shape of its architecture start to explain each other.

Empirical findings

1,204 pull requests across four authorship conditions; 12 follow-up interviews.

Key finding
68%

of reviewers re-read agent-authored diffs at least twice before approving

  • 2.4× longer on unfamiliar files
  • 41% asked for a written rationale
  • 1 in 5 escalated to a second reviewer
