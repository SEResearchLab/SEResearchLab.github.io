About Us

The SEResearch Lab explores the human and social dimensions of software engineering. We study team collaboration dynamics, examine AI's impact on technical practices, and work to enhance the experience of the ones who build technology.

🤝

Socio-Technical Flow

Understanding developer wellbeing, psychological safety, and friction-free cognitive workflows in modern teams.

🧠

Human-AI Symbiosis

Investigating agentic workflows, prompt-to-verify shifts, and how copilots alter programming craft.

✨

Inclusion & Diversity

Championing neurodiverse software engineers, ADHD inclusion, and accessible development environments.
