About us

The SEResearch Lab explores the human and social dimensions of software engineering. We study team collaboration dynamics, examine AI's impact on technical practices, and work to enhance the experience of the ones who build technology.

Developer Experience & AI

DevEx and ADHD

Communication, coordination, and inclusion challenges for developers with ADHD, in collaboration with PUCRS.

Creative Problem-Solving in SE

Join Us

We welcome undergraduate and graduate students interested in the human and social side of software engineering — developer experience, human-AI collaboration, and diversity and inclusion in tech. If this sounds like your kind of research, get in touch with a short note about your interests and, if available, a CV.
